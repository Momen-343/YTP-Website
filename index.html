Save exact timestamps instantly with dedicated buttons built right into the YouTube player.

    Features:

  • Bookmark any moment: Save exact timestamps instantly on any YouTube video using quick-access buttons built directly into the player interface.
  • Add custom notes: Attach personalized text notes to any saved timestamp so you always remember the context behind your bookmark.
  • Jump to timestamps instantly: Click on any saved marker or list item to skip directly to that precise second in the video.
  • Interactive seekbar markers: View all your saved bookmarks rendered as glowing visual markers directly on the YouTube progress bar.
  • Dedicated history tab: Access a clean, organized side panel listing all the videos you've bookmarked, making it easy to manage and revisit past content.
  • No YouTube login required: Keep your workflow private and independent—all data stays safely stored right inside your browser.
